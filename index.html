You already build SAML. Auth0 wants 400%+ more to let your customers turn it on.

If you're a mid-market B2B SaaS team who just landed your first enterprise deal, you've probably run into the "SSO tax" — the moment your auth vendor gates single sign-on behind their most expensive plan, right when a real customer is asking for it.

This isn't a one-off complaint

The "SSO tax" is a named, community-documented pattern that's been called out for years — not something we're inventing to sell you a fix.

"sso.tax" maintains a public "Wall of Shame" of vendors that gate SSO behind a >10% price premium — and a "Friends of SSO" list of vendors that don't.

What we're exploring

A lighter-weight way for mid-market SaaS teams to offer SSO to their own customers — without eating (or passing along) the usual enterprise-identity-vendor markup. We haven't built a finished product yet. Before we do, we want to talk to people who've actually lived this.

Have you eaten an SSO tax, or had to explain one to a customer?

We're doing a handful of short conversations (15–20 min, async written replies also welcome) with people who own auth/identity decisions at a B2B SaaS company. No pitch — we want to hear what actually happened: which vendor, what it cost, what you did about it.
